- [Bugfix] Mount the `openedx-media` and `openedx-media-private` volumes in the `lms-job` and `cms-job` services, like in the `lms` and `cms` services. Files written by jobs to the media folders were lost when the job container was removed: for instance, `importdemocourse` left the demo course with video transcripts whose files did not exist, so that transcript requests failed with a 500 error. To restore the demo course transcripts, run `tutor local do importdemocourse` again. (by @Faraz32123)
